How the value is built — and a playground to test it

implied = TVL × 0.20 × (1 + growth × weight) × (0.70 + 0.45 × √(age ÷ 1460 days)) × (1 + 0.04 × extra chains, max 5)

Growth is clamped to −60%…+120% and missing growth counts as 0. Age is days since the snapshot first tracked the protocol; unknown age counts as 365 days. Move a slider and the board re-ranks live, from the same figures — the ranking you see is the model, not a fixed list.
